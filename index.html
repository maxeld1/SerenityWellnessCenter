About Serenity & Helen

Helen is the heart of a small, highly personal practice.

Serenity Wellness Center is built around careful listening and a calm, unhurried presence. Clients describe Helen as knowledgeable, genuinely caring, and the reason many of them stopped going anywhere else.

Sessions can be focused and therapeutic, quiet and restorative, or a mix of both. The pace is steady, and the care is shaped around the person in the room.
